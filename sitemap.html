Feature sitemap · August 2026

Every surface, from every seat.

Download the PDF ↓

VYB is one product with eight seats. Each seat gets the same shell, its own surface, its own agent, and a capability scope that is narrow by default. This page lists all of it — what every persona can open, and what they can do there.

01

The shell every seat gets

HomeThe seat's day: decisions waiting on you, tonight, the seat's own cards, shortcuts.
CalThe calendar — the tour for the crew, the room's dates for an operator.
Talk · VYB VoiceThe orb. Talk to VYB; the brief comes back in the seat's own terms.
InboxWaiting on you · auto-applied · threads. Badge = what actually needs your tap.
MeYour seat: your agent, your surface, tasks assigned to you, every surface you may open, audit & reports, demo controls.
Concierge · iMessage@vyb in a thread → a proposal card → tap-confirm → every screen updates. Money never executes from a message.
Your vybEvery seat gets its own vyb — it texts you at hours it has learned, with provenance, and asks — never acts.
Between teamsYour agent talking to another team's agent. Agents negotiate; humans confirm; every shared fact carries the permission it crossed under.
Audit trail & reportsEverything consequential, scoped to what the seat may read. Exports are audited.

06

The rules under every surface

Money is never auto-executedEvery money action is an explicit in-app tap-confirm. The confirm is the legal record.
Scope is narrow by defaultCapabilities are allow-listed per seat. Least access unless a membership says otherwise.
Everything consequential is auditedGrants, confirms, revokes, money, exports — an append-only trail, scoped to what the seat may read.
PrivacyConcierge messages ≤30 days. PII envelope-encrypted. Venue reviews never publicly attributed.
One source of truthSwift state machines mirror the SQL. The demo runs entirely on seeded data; the live store is one config flip.
