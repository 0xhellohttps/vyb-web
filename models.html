What a live-music operator looks like as a payment business.

Six outside-in models of what live-music operators and festivals of different shapes could represent as payment businesses. Every figure is an estimate built from published capacities, attendance and typical event spending — not from any operator's books.

The subjects are anonymized on purpose. Operator is a multi-venue operator running four rooms; Festival A is a city-scale festival week, Festival B a festival and cultural network, and Festivals C–E are a greenfield launch, a two-city festival and a festival with permanent venues. Room 1–4 are the four venues in the operator's portfolio.

The final chart shows how VYB would allocate a seed round against this opportunity.

Illustrative models only. No company is named, none has been contacted, reviewed or endorsed this, and nothing here states any real business's actual finances. Figures are hypothetical and would have to be validated against real books.
