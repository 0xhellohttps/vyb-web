04 — THE TERMS

What we don't take.

A development program that quietly takes a piece of the artist isn't development. It's a deal with a nicer name.

0%equity
No stake in youNo equity in your business, no share of your masters, no participation in your publishing or catalog. You own what you make, exactly as you did before.
100%non-recoupable
Nothing is paid backThe grant is philanthropic support, not an advance. It isn't recouped from touring, streaming or any other income. There's no balance to work off.
2separate entities
Structurally separateGrants are made through an appropriately structured non-profit, held deliberately separate from VYB's commercial business — so support stays support, and the two are never confused.

VYB earns the ordinary way — payment processing, software and services, on the same commercial terms as any other customer. We do well when your business grows, not by taking a piece of it.

06 — WHERE IT GOES

A pipeline, not a one-off.

The intention is to stay with artists as they grow, rather than fund one moment and disappear. Over time VYB Pulse is designed to run as three connected tiers.

Emerging
~3 artists
$10K–$15K grants
The entry cohort — capital, infrastructure and the first real view of the business.
Breakthrough
~5 artists
$25K–$50K grants
Artists with traction, scaling touring and team — where routing and cash flow start to decide everything.
Vanguard
1–3 artists
$50K–$100K+ project funding
Ambitious projects that need real production capital behind them.

Alongside the cohorts: fellowships, residencies, educational programming, technology access, and partnerships with festivals, universities and cultural institutions.

07 — RESEARCH

The VYB Artist Economy Report.

The economics of live music are badly documented — which is precisely why they stay unfair. VYB intends to publish an annual report drawn from anonymized, aggregated activity across the network.

08 — PARTNERS

Built to be funded by more than one party.

VYB seeds the program, but it's designed as a capital stack — so that partner support multiplies what reaches artists rather than replacing it.

Strategic sponsors

Financial institutions, technology and cloud companies, audio and hardware brands, consumer, fashion, telecom and hospitality partners.

Festivals & cultural institutions

Curatorial partners who already know how to develop artists, and can offer the cohort stages, market access and international exposure.

Public & philanthropic

Cultural funding bodies, universities, foundations and individual donors adding grant capital to the pool.

The ambition is straightforward: a modest VYB commitment, matched several times over by partners who care about the same outcome, turning into a program that is meaningfully larger than anything VYB would fund alone.
