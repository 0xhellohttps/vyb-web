The pitch · September 2026

Agentic intelligence for music.

VYB puts a persistent agent — a vyb — in every seat of live music. It watches the whole operation, catches what's slipping, and finishes the work; the app is its control plane. Never miss a beat.

01 · Problem

A tour is a hundred moving parts, run out of a group chat.

Holds, offers, routing, tickets, travel, crew, merch, releases — every one of them a commitment with an owner and a clock, living in threads where things slip.

The hold lapses FridayNobody replied. The date goes to another act.
The counter sits three daysThe routing window closes around it.
The merch PO slipsThe lead time no longer lands before the drop date.

Every slip costs money, momentum, or a relationship. And every existing tool waits to be asked.

02 · The shift

Software waits to be asked. Agents finish work.

The last generation of music tools were dashboards — they showed you the work. The agent generation does the work: watches continuously, decides within permissions, executes with a human tap on anything that matters.

Persistent agency, not chat. A team never has to ask its vyb what's happening — the vyb tells them, before it mattered.

03 · Product

Every seat gets its vyb.

Artist · DJ · photographer · manager · tour manager · booking agent · venue operator — one product, seven seats, each with an agent scoped to that seat's capabilities and day.

It watchesCalendar, holds and offers, the chat, drop and PO dates — the operation's own record, not a scraped feed.
It catchesThe unit of value is the caught beat: surfaced with evidence, before the human noticed, with the finished next action attached.
It finishesPropose → confirm → execute. The follow-up chased, the schedule moved, the paper advanced — with your tap on anything consequential.

North-star metric: confirmed catches per team per week. Every confirm or dismissal is a labeled data point — the product's own success metric builds its moat.

04 · Creation, not just vigilance

Rollout — the campaign, planned by two vybs.

The artist's vyb is a social index of what's moving across platforms and an ideation partner — an encyclopedia of eccentric references that helps the artist take an abstract concept from zero to one, populating a board of references and directions, then adding logic. The creativity stays the artist's. The desk's vyb is the realization guardian: once both seats sign the treatment, every content beat becomes an owned, dated task with watchers armed.

Social indexTrends, sounds, formats and cultural moments, indexed against this act's audience — with the driver behind each one.
IdeationBring an idea, however abstract — the vyb adds references and logic, and you take it zero to one together. Taste trains on every pick and dismissal; the creativity stays the artist's.
Actionable on agreementBoth signatures compile the plan into work. Spend lines confirm at the desk — always.
05 · The network

Your vyb finds another vyb on the other side.

A horizontal agent emails a promoter and hopes. VYB holds both sides: the desk's vyb and the room's vyb negotiate settlement terms, work the two-night option, place the merch — agents negotiate, humans confirm. Every seat added is a counterpart for all the rest.

07 · The moat

Dashboards don't compound. Agent memory does.

The seat graphBoth sides of every negotiation on one protocol — a network effect at the agent layer, not the payment layer.
Domain stateA generalist starts from a blank browser. A vyb starts from the operation's own record: holds, routing, terms, settled history.
The labeled recordEvery confirm and dismissal is a labeled example of what matters in music ops — and of each act's taste. An eval set nobody else can assemble.
Memory as switching costThe vyb that has watched your operation for a year is irreplaceable. Leaving means losing institutional memory.

09 · The transaction rung

Revenue scales with the agency teams grant.

When the vybs execute — campaign spend, crew travel, merch purchase orders, all under tap-confirm — VYB takes a share of managed spend (target 6%). The more a team trusts its vyb to finish, the more VYB earns. Revenue is aligned with agency, before a dollar of payments infrastructure.

agent → orchestration → transaction → financial infrastructure — Stripe remains the rail underneath, initially. Own the decisions that cause the money to move, and the money layer becomes the natural place those decisions execute.

10 · The other side of the show

Venues are mapped. Artists and labels lead.

The operator lane — room intelligence, the booking desk, settlement and the reconciliation run — is built and its monetization ladder is understood (connect → intelligence → settlement). This raise leads with the artist and label side, where the agent story is sharpest and the network's gravity lives; the operator lane converts as the counterpart of every artist-side negotiation.
